Jennifer Kumura is a Honolulu-based strategy and transformation leader with over 10 years of experience in human-centered design, now expanding into organizational strategy and change leadership. Currently Digital Transformation Lead at Servco Pacific Inc., she also co-founded and leads UXHI, Hawaiʻi's largest UX and product design community. Rooted in her Okinawan heritage, she leads with chimugukuru (heart and sincerity), yuimaaru (collective collaboration), and stewardship, values that shape how she builds teams, solves problems, and invests in community.

Her focus areas span digital product strategy, organizational development, and change management, with a particular strength in leveraging technology to improve process and operations, translating business goals into scalable systems that help organizations work smarter. She is increasingly drawn to helping Hawaiʻi organizations integrate human-centered thinking into how they operate, not just as a design method, but as a driver of strategy, culture, and decision-making.

Jennifer holds a B.S. in Human-Centered Design & Engineering from the University of Washington and is a DLEMBA candidate at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa's Shidler College of Business, expected Spring 2028. She has led design teams at organizations including GE Digital and Formation, built partnerships across 12+ Hawaiʻi organizations through UXHI, and serves on the boards of the Honolulu Japanese Junior Chamber of Commerce and Hawaii United Okinawa Association.

In the near term, Jennifer is pursuing leadership roles in organizational effectiveness and change management. Long term, she aims to help Hawaiʻi organizations balance operational excellence with empathy and human insight, strengthening the systems and communities she calls home.
